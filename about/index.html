About Us

Built for public purchasing

Avenza Technologies LLC works with state agencies and institutions across South Carolina to source, coordinate, and deliver the equipment and supplies their contracts require.

Who we are

Avenza Technologies LLC is a South Carolina-based wholesale supplier serving state government and institutional buyers. We operate from Charleston and are registered as a South Carolina state vendor.

Our work sits between the buyer and the supply chain. Agencies tell us what a contract requires; we build relationships with manufacturers and distributors to deliver it at reliable pricing, on a schedule the contract can depend on.

What we value

Responsiveness. Quotes come back quickly and questions get answered by a person who knows the order.

Accuracy. Specifications, quantities, and delivery terms are confirmed in writing before anything ships.

Follow-through. An order is not finished until it is delivered, documented, and invoiced correctly.

Why buyers work with us

Public contracts have requirements that a general supplier is not always set up to meet.

We know the paperwork

Solicitations, purchase orders, and invoicing requirements are part of the job, not an afterthought.

Local presence

South Carolina operations mean shorter lines of communication and easier coordination on delivery.

Supplier depth

Direct manufacturer and distributor relationships give us options when lead times or pricing get tight.

One point of contact

You deal with the same people from quote through close-out. Nothing gets handed off and lost.

Let's talk about your requirement

Tell us what you need sourced and we will come back with pricing and availability.
