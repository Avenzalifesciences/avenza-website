Avenza Technologies LLC helps small and growing businesses reach more customers, sell more, and understand their numbers.

Who we are

Avenza Technologies LLC is a digital growth company based in Charleston, South Carolina. We work with business owners who know their product but do not have the time or the team to market it online.

We step in as your marketing strategist, web developer, video creator, social media manager and data analyst, all in one team. We modernize outdated websites, build new ones for businesses that do not have one, put your products in front of buyers on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, and give you dashboards that show how sales and stock are moving.
