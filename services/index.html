Services

Sourcing, fulfillment, and supplier management

We handle the supply side of public and institutional contracts so your team can stay focused on the work the contract is actually for.

Equipment & Supply Sourcing

We locate products that match your specifications and budget, confirm availability, and return written quotes with lead times. When an exact item is unavailable, we propose compliant alternates rather than leaving you to search.

Government Contract Fulfillment

From a single purchase order to a recurring multi-year contract, we place orders, track production and shipping, coordinate delivery, and handle invoicing and documentation through close-out.

Vendor & Supplier Management

We maintain direct relationships with manufacturers and distributors, which gives us room to negotiate pricing, compare lead times, and find a second source when the first one slips.

Quoting & Bid Support

Need pricing to build a solicitation response or budget request? We provide documented quotes with part numbers, lead times, and terms you can submit directly.

Recurring Supply Programs

For consumables and repeat-order items, we set up scheduled deliveries so stock arrives before it runs out, with pricing held for the contract term.

Compliance & Documentation

Certificates, specification sheets, delivery confirmations, and invoicing formatted to meet the requirements attached to the contract.

Process

From requirement to close-out

Every engagement follows the same four steps, so you always know what happens next and what we need from you.

  1. 1

    Review the requirement

    We read the solicitation or purchase order and confirm specifications, quantities, and delivery terms before quoting.

  2. 2

    Source and quote

    We go to our supplier network for pricing and availability, then return a written quote with lead times.

  3. 3

    Fulfill and deliver

    On award, we place the order, track it, and coordinate delivery to your site or receiving facility.

  4. 4

    Close out

    We handle invoicing, documentation, and any follow-up the contract requires.
